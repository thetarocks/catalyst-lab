What the model can establish

Single-round applicant DSIC is established theory under the standard DA assumptions. The Python audit observed zero profitable deviations across 1,080 selected preference changes in one synthetic market; a finite check does not prove the theorem.

The teaching model uses strict common priorities and simple capacities. The separate quota fixture checks merit-first OPEN selection and category fallback at one program. It does not model every official JoSAA rule or establish general quota monotonicity. No real submitted student preferences are used.
