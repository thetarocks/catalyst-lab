ABOUT ME / A RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Learning by
making rules run.

I’m a high school student interested in computer science, economics and the rules that connect them. I built Your Catalyst to make those questions concrete: who gets a scarce seat, how a voting rule distributes influence, and what happens when a market model meets its assumptions.

These projects are helping me distinguish a known theoretical result from what a finite simulation can establish. I’m working on making the inputs, code and evidence easy to inspect, so a reader can question a result rather than take a chart on trust.

Next, I want to test richer preference models in the allocation audit, make the governance data easier to reconstruct, and study identity costs in the Sybil model. I also want to learn from people using the simulators; I’ll add feedback and outside validation when there is something concrete to cite.
