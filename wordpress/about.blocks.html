HOW I APPROACH A QUESTION

Build. Inspect. Reconsider.

01

Write down the rules.

Define the agents, the information they have and the constraints they face. Make the assumptions visible.

02

Make the mechanism run.

Translate the rules into code. Follow individual trades, payments or allocations, rather than only the final chart.

03

Look for the boundary.

Change one assumption. Compare the outcome, inspect numerical checks and ask what the model leaves out.

RESEARCH INTERESTS / OPEN A QUESTION

Three threads I keep pulling.

01   Market design

How should a clearinghouse allocate scarce places when participants have different preferences and eligibility rules? My interests include two-sided matching, Gale–Shapley deferred acceptance, quota interleaving and dynamic vacancy chains.

02   Computational social choice

How does a voting rule distribute influence, and what happens if a participant can split resources across identities? I’m interested in quadratic voting, Sybil resistance, capital concentration and governance mechanisms in decentralized organizations.

Read the governance work ↗
03   Information economics

How do you fund something that people can benefit from without paying for it? I’m interested in public goods, the free-rider problem, non-excludable information goods and quadratic matching-grant mechanisms.
