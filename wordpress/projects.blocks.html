THE PROJECT INDEX

Experiments & projects.

A collection of interactive models and mechanism-design work. Start with a question, inspect the assumptions, and follow the calculations through to the result.

Six questions to explore.

VIEW THE NOTEBOOK ↗

Interactive models with adjustable inputs, reproducible runs, downloadable results and explanations of the mathematics. All six use synthetic scenarios, with assumptions and limitations stated inside each experiment.

01 / MARKETS

The price of immediacy ↗

A price–time matching engine that exposes every fill. Change order size, spread and depth to see why the quoted price is only part of the cost.

Matching engine · Fill ledger · VWAP

02 / DEFI

Liquidity has a curve ↗

Trace a constant-product swap through the reserves, then compare liquidity provision with holding. Separate fee income, execution shortfall and divergence loss.

Reserve accounting · Price impact · LP returns

03 / DERIVATIVES

A hedge is a process ↗

Sell a call and finance a delta hedge through 600 simulated stock paths. Explore the trade-off between rebalancing error, transaction costs and a wrong volatility assumption.

Monte Carlo · Black–Scholes · Self-financing hedge

04 / PORTFOLIOS

Beyond the tidy frontier ↗

Build a long-only portfolio, change the correlation structure and compare Gaussian with heavy-tailed shocks. The same volatility can conceal a different tail.

Covariance · Grid frontier · Expected shortfall

05 / NETWORKS

When one balance sheet breaks ↗

Send an asset shock through eight connected banks. Compare ring, complete and hub networks using proportional clearing and inspect which defaults are secondary.

Fixed-point solver · Network topology · Contagion

06 / MECHANISMS

Who pays for the winning bid? ↗

Run first-price and second-price auctions on the same 10,000 value draws. Test reserve-aware equilibrium bids, then move bidding away from equilibrium.

Auction theory · Paired estimators · Revenue equivalence

RESEARCH IN PROGRESS

Sybil reversal thresholds in quadratic voting

Quadratic voting changes the relationship between spending and influence. The governance work examines how splitting resources across identities can change that relationship, using counterfactual simulations around DAO voting.

A note on the work.

A model is useful when its assumptions are visible. Each new experiment includes a worked example, primary references and numerical checks. The simulations illustrate established ideas; they do not establish new empirical findings. Code and implementation notes, including the use of AI coding assistance, are available in the repository.
